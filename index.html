OpenClaw Calendar

A personal, single-user assistant integration for Google Workspace.

OpenClaw Calendar is a private automation used by one person to connect their own Google account (Calendar, Gmail, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Contacts and Tasks) to a self-hosted personal assistant running on hardware they own.

It is not a public service. There is no sign-up and no other users. The assistant runs on the owner's own device; to do its job it sends the content it works with to two processors chosen by the owner — Anthropic (the Claude language model, via the owner's own account) and Telegram (the owner's own messaging account). Nothing is stored on any server operated by this integration.
