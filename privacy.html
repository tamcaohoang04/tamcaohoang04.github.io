Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 September 2026

Who this covers

OpenClaw Calendar is a personal integration operated and used by a single individual (the owner) to connect their own Google account to a self-hosted assistant. It has no other users.

What data is accessed

With the owner's explicit consent via Google OAuth, the application may read and write data in the owner's own Google account, limited to the scopes granted: Google Calendar, Gmail, Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Sheets, Google Contacts and Google Tasks.

How data is used

Data is used solely to provide assistant features to the owner — for example reading upcoming events, creating calendar entries, reading email to extract dates, and reading or updating spreadsheets the owner chooses. Data is processed on hardware owned and controlled by the owner.

Storage and sharing

OAuth tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's own device. No Google user data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or transferred to any external service other than as required to fulfil the owner's own requests (for example, sending a summary the owner asked for to the owner's own messaging account).

Google API Services User Data Policy

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access deletes the application's ability to reach the account; locally stored tokens are then invalid and can be removed from the device.
