Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 September 2026 (rev. 2)

Who this covers

OpenClaw Calendar is a personal integration operated and used by a single individual (the owner) to connect their own Google account to a self-hosted assistant. It has no other users.

What data is accessed

With the owner's explicit consent via Google OAuth, the application may read and write data in the owner's own Google account, limited to the scopes granted: Google Calendar, Gmail, Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Sheets, Google Contacts and Google Tasks.

How data is used

Data is used solely to provide assistant features to the owner — for example reading upcoming events, creating calendar entries, reading email to extract dates, and reading or updating spreadsheets the owner chooses. Data is processed on hardware owned and controlled by the owner.

Storage and sharing

OAuth tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's own device. No Google user data is sold, used for advertising, or shared with any third party except the two processors below, and only as far as needed to carry out the owner's own requests.

Processors (sub-processors)

  • Anthropic — the Claude language model, accessed through the owner's own Anthropic account. Excerpts of Google data (for example an email's text or a list of calendar events) are sent to it so the assistant can answer the owner's request. Anthropic's handling is governed by its own privacy terms for that account.
  • Telegram — the owner's own messaging account, used to deliver the assistant's replies and summaries to the owner.

Retention and deletion

Google data is read on demand and is not copied into a database of its own. Short-lived copies may exist on the owner's device in assistant conversation history and encrypted device backups, both under the owner's sole control and deletable by the owner at any time. Nothing is retained on servers operated by this integration, because there are none.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access deletes the application's ability to reach the account; locally stored tokens are then invalid and can be removed from the device.
